# Louvre-Slide Door

A step-free sliding door whose four wooden slats turn like blinds (airflow + privacy), then the whole frame slides along an overhead track into a parking bay beside the opening. It needs no swing space and has no floor step.

Size: opening 900 x 2100 mm; whole assembly 1925 x 2135 x 150 mm.

## Parts (30)
- **Frame/track:** floor guide track, threshold, left/right jambs, head rail, parking wall
- **Sliding frame:** top bar, bottom bar, 2 stiles, 2 hanger rods, guide shoe
- **Louvres:** 4 timber slats, each with a brush seal and pivot pin
- **Controls:** control plate, red louvre dial, pull handle + 2 posts

## What moves
- **Louvre dial** (0-90 deg) turns all 4 slats together (linked with `follows`).
- **Door slide** (0-880 mm) slides the frame along the head rail.

## Assumptions
Slats are 26 mm timber; dial coupling simplified (real: cord/gear); no lock modelled. Print the slats and frame parts flat; the model has a flat base on y = 0.
