## 2. Rules to Check Against

- **50/30/20 check:** Needs ≈ 50% of income, Wants ≈ 30%, Savings ≈ 20%. With the example numbers, Needs total 1,600 (53%), Wants 250 (8%), Savings 400 (13%).
- **Target:** Left over should be **≥ 0**. Any negative value means cutting a category before the month starts.
- **Savings first:** Move the savings rows to the top of your payday routine so they are paid before discretionary spending.
